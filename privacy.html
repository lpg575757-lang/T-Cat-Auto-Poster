Privacy Policy

This policy explains how T Cat Auto Poster handles information and how planned account integrations will be governed.

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Public contact address

For privacy, data, or service inquiries, contact t4786366@gmail.com.

What information may be processed

This informational website does not currently collect account credentials, access tokens, video files, or post metadata. If interactive features are introduced, T Cat Auto Poster may process the minimum account authorization data, user-selected video information, and publishing details needed to provide the requested service.

TikTok OAuth authentication

Before any TikTok OAuth feature is made available, users will be redirected to TikTok's authorization flow and asked to approve the requested permissions. T Cat Auto Poster will not ask users to enter their TikTok password into this website.

Access tokens

No TikTok access or refresh tokens are stored by the current static website. Before OAuth integration is released, tokens will be handled only in a secure non-public environment, protected from unauthorized access, and used only for actions authorized by the user.

Video and post metadata

Future publishing features may process user-selected filenames, titles, descriptions, privacy choices, upload status, and platform response identifiers. The policy will be updated before such processing begins.

How information is used

Information will be used only to authenticate an authorized user, prepare user-requested publishing actions, provide status information, maintain security, and comply with applicable obligations. It will not be sold.

Data storage

The current website has no account database and uses no cookies or browser storage. Any future storage location, protection measures, and retention rules will be documented before data collection starts.

Third-party services

Future account and publishing functionality may interact with TikTok or other supported platforms only when a user chooses that integration. Those services process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

TikTok API

If enabled after review and testing, TikTok API access will be limited to the permissions granted by the user and the functions needed for the user's requested action. T Cat Auto Poster is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards will be applied before any account data is processed. Secrets and tokens will never be placed in public HTML, client-side JavaScript, or a public source repository.

Data retention

The current static website retains no user account data. Before interactive features launch, retention periods will be limited to what is necessary for the service, security, and legal requirements and will be stated here.

Your requests and deletion

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with the service by contacting the address below. Requests will be verified and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will be presented before they take effect.
