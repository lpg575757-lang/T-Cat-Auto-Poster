About

Organize first. Publish with confidence.

T Cat Auto Poster is a creator tool designed to help users manage video content they own and prepare it for publishing to supported platforms. It is intended for original or properly authorized content—not for copying, scraping, or publishing someone else's work.

How it works

A clear, creator-controlled flow

  1. 01Prepare your own video

    Select content you own or have permission to publish.

  2. 02Review publishing details

    Check the title, description, and intended audience.

  3. 03Authorize your account

    Use the supported platform's authorization process.

  4. 04Publish with explicit user consent

    Confirm the final details before content is sent.

Data and authorization

Your account stays under your control.

  • We do not access a TikTok account without the account holder's authorization.
  • T Cat Auto Poster does not collect your TikTok password.
  • OAuth authorization is planned for supported account connections.
  • Only permissions needed for the requested publishing action will be requested.
  • Only content deliberately selected and managed by the user is in scope.

Integration status

TikTok Direct Post is currently being integrated.

The public website is available for service information. OAuth and content-publishing features are not yet available and must be implemented and tested separately before a Production review. T Cat Auto Poster is an independent service and is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok.
